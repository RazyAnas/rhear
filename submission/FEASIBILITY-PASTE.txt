FEASIBILITY	EVIDENCE	
Technical	97.4 KB int8 against a 200 KB cap; ~15% of one core. Model trained and benchmarked, neural VAD running on the ESP32-S3, adaptive canceller validated at +12.6 dB	Proven
Financial	India-sourced BOM ₹6,370; ₹4,071 as a retrofit. ~₹20,000 fielded against ₹1.8 lakh for the reference system. iDEX and TDF are funded routes	Costed
Market	22.9% of Indian Air Force personnel show noise-induced hearing loss, 26.18% in technical trades. The reference system reached ~20,000 soldiers worldwide, its price cited as the limit	Unmet need
Operational	Retrofits into an in-service earmuff: no new shell, no re-qualification of certified passive attenuation. Every part from Indian distributors, none export-controlled	Low friction


===== BLOCK 2 — VIABILITY (2 columns x 6 rows) =====
CHALLENGE	HOW WE SOLVE IT
Quality falls short at the harshest noise levels	We measured the ceiling and proved a perfect single-microphone mask cannot reach the target. A second boom microphone separates the wearer from a bystander with 100% reliability at +10.1 dB, using wavefront curvature alone — no voice enrolment, one extra component on the same data bus, validated across 2,160 acoustic scenes
Running the full network on a microcontroller	Memory and compute are not the constraint: 97 KB of a 200 KB budget, 15% of one core. The largest and most error-prone block is already written in C and matches the reference implementation to −133.5 dB
Active cancellation demands very low latency	The 146 µs budget is geometry, not preference. The codec was selected on its 38 µs datasheet figure and the circuit is complete and electrically verified at 37 parts and 30 nets
Ruggedisation and environmental qualification	The retrofit scope keeps the certified passive shell untouched, so only the electronics require qualification — a far smaller and cheaper path than qualifying a new headset
Siren coverage in training data	The loader is already in our codebase from an earlier build, so this is a dataset rebuild rather than new research. We report only what we have measured
